# Executive-Retail-Analytics-PowerBI
An interactive Power BI dashboard providing executive-level retail performance overview with KPI cards, sales trends, revenue breakdown by category, top product rankings, store location map, drill-through transaction reports, custom tooltip panels, and monthly slicer filters.

# Features

-KPI Cards — Total Revenue (1.34M), Total Quantity, Total Customers, and Average Revenue at a glance with sparkline backgrounds

-Revenue Breakdown by Category — Donut chart showing Electronics (71%), Home & Kitchen (16%), Fashion (5%), Beauty (2%), and Sports (4%)

-Sales Trend Over Time — Monthly bar chart revealing seasonality and revenue peaks

-Top Performing Products — Horizontal bar chart ranking Smart TV, iPhone 15, Laptop, Samsung Galaxy S24, Gaming Console, and Air Fryer by revenue

-Store Location Map — Bing Maps integration showing store presence across major Indian cities (Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Jaipur, Lucknow)

-Top Customer Analysis — Visual ranking of customers by total spending

-Month Slicer — Interactive filter to slice all visuals by month

-Custom Tooltip Panel — Hover interaction revealing Top 3 Products, Location Revenue donut, Category Quantity, and Average Unit Price

-Drill-Through Report — Detailed transaction-level table with Month, Customer ID, Product, Quantity, Revenue, Category, Unit Price, Customer Type, Payment Method, and Store — with grand total.
# Tools & Technologies

-This project was built using a combination of four powerful tools working together in a complete data pipeline.

-Microsoft Excel was used for raw data storage and initial data preparation, providing the foundational dataset for the entire project.

-MySQL was used for data querying, cleaning, and structured data management — ensuring the data was accurate, consistent, and ready for analysis before moving to the next stage.

-Python was used for data preprocessing, transformation, and exploratory analysis — handling tasks like data cleaning automation, formatting, and preparing the final dataset for visualization.

-Power BI Desktop was used to build the complete interactive dashboard — including DAX measures for KPI calculations, custom tooltip panels, drill-through reports, dynamic slicers, and Bing Maps integration for store location visualization.

# Dataset Overview

The dataset (Retails_data) includes the following fields:

-Month — Transaction month

-Customer_ID — Unique customer identifier

-Product — Product name

-Category — Product category (Electronics, Fashion, Sports, etc.)

-Quantity — Units sold

-Revenue — Total revenue generated

-Unit_Price — Price per unit

-Customer_Type — New or Returning

-Payment_Method — Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card, UPI, Net Banking

-Store_Location — City where the transaction occurred

# Dataset summary:

-Total Revenue: ₹1.34M

-Total Quantity: 2,813

-Total Customers: 950+

-Average Revenue: ₹15.79K

# Key Insights

-Electronics dominates revenue at 71% of total category share

-Smart TV is the top revenue-generating product, followed by iPhone 15 and Laptop

-October records the highest monthly sales peak

-Stores are spread across 7 major Indian cities

-Both New and Returning customers are tracked, enabling retention analysis

-Multiple payment methods (UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, Cash, Net Banking) are captured
